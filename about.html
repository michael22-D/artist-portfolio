MEET THE ARTIST

Derrick Akuamoah

Derrick Akuamoah is a Ghanaian abstract mixed-media artist based in Accra, Ghana. His artistic practice explores the overlooked marks, textures, and material traces found within everyday environments, examining how surfaces record time, movement, human activity, and memory.

Working primarily with acrylic, charcoal, pastel, and mixed-media materials, Akuamoah approaches painting as an ongoing process of investigation, discovery, and transformation. Rather than beginning with predetermined images, he allows the accumulation of layers, gestures, scratches, stains, erasures, and textured surfaces to guide each composition.

His work invites viewers to reconsider overlooked details and recognise the significance of marks that often go unnoticed. Even the most ordinary surface can hold traces of experience, memory, and human existence.

Artistic Philosophy

“Nothing truly disappears; everything leaves a trace”.

Ordinary and overlooked surfaces carry silent stories of memory, time, and human presence. Through abstraction and mixed-media experimentation, I reinterpret scratches, stains, cracks, and weathered textures as expressions of memory, perception, and presence.

Creative Process

I build layers through adding, covering, scraping, removing, and revealing. As the painting progresses, I allow materials and spontaneous interactions to guide it. The process is as significant as the finished work: observation, experimentation, and discovery.
